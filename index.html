Use Edit to paste TLE/OMM, or Export satellites.json and run GitHub Actions. Revisit interval is NOT necessarily the ground-track repeat cycle.

🛰️ Days 1–16: TLE / SGP4 geometric screening · Day 17+: configured ground-track repeat proxy (reference angles and approximate UTC; LOW confidence). Export 30 Days / 1 Year includes method and TLE epoch per row.
Important: SGP4 candidate = geometric proximity estimate only. TLE does not provide attitude, imaging command, cloud-free image, sensor swath or verified footprint. Time resolution is not forecast accuracy; 16-day TLE projections may drift, and repeat-cycle times/angles are low-confidence proxies; this is NOT a 1-second accuracy claim. A TLE newer than 72h is required at calculation start, but propagation extends to 16 days for exploratory screening (accuracy progressively degrades). After day 16, a repeat-cycle phase template may be used where separately configured, including optional historical backcast as low-confidence seed data; do not treat repeated times or angles as exact. No satellite attitude, slew or authorized acquisition is inferred. A single TLE cannot reliably give exact imaging times for 30 days or 1 year.
